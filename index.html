Who we are

Working alongside communities since 2005

Based in Lucknow, Madhayam Samajik Sanstha has been working since 2005 with people and communities facing barriers to opportunity. Our approach is simple: listen carefully, work locally and build solutions that can last.

We believe lasting change is built with people, not simply delivered to them. That means creating space for women, children and families to shape priorities, strengthen skills and make decisions about their own futures. From a local health camp to a longer-term livelihood programme, the relationship starts with listening.

What we do

Practical work, close to home

From education and health to skills, livelihoods and women’s empowerment, our programmes respond to everyday needs and the people who experience them first-hand.

01

Women empowerment

Field initiatives focused on gender equality, financial independence and social rights, supporting over 2,000 women across Lucknow's urban slums.

02

Education & literacy

Formal and non-formal educational opportunities for underprivileged children, helping them join the mainstream of development.

03

Health & nutrition

Local health check-up camps and awareness campaigns on nutrition and HIV/AIDS prevention, reaching families where they live.
