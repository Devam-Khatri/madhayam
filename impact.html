What our work has reached so far

These figures span our work across Uttar Pradesh since 2005, and will be updated as our record keeping continues to improve.

4,000+ Children supported through our education programmes
700+ Young people trained in vocational and technical skills
65+ Self-help groups mobilised through our micro-finance work
600+ Farmers reached through agriculture and rural development
800+ People reached through health and nutrition camps
500+ Citizens supported through our advocacy and RTI work

“Our vision is to attain a healthy and self-reliant community by nurturing integration, unity, peace and harmony. Every figure on this page, present or still to be added, is a step toward that.”
