Madhayam Gallery photo guide

Put your real gallery photographs in this folder using the exact filenames shown beside each placeholder in gallery.html.

Gallery archive years, newest first:
2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020, 2018, 2017, 2013, 2011, 2010

Each year currently has two image slots:
  gallery-2026-01.jpg
  gallery-2026-02.jpg
  gallery-2025-01.jpg
  gallery-2025-02.jpg
  ...
  gallery-2010-01.jpg
  gallery-2010-02.jpg

The filename identifies the year only. Do not assume the photograph subject from the filename. In gallery.html, edit the title/caption/theme to match the actual photograph.

Useful photograph subjects for this archive are:
- children enjoying or studying
- women empowerment
- food distribution
- supplies distribution
- health or sanitary item distribution

Recommended image shape: 3:2 landscape. Good-quality originals are preferred.

If you have more than two photographs for a year, duplicate a year-photo figure in that year's section and give the new image a sequential filename such as gallery-2024-03.jpg.
