Privacy policy

How this website handles information you choose to share with Madhayam Samajik Sanstha.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Information you provide

If you contact us through the website, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number and the message you submit. We use this information to respond to enquiries, partnership requests and other communications.

How we use information

We use submitted information only for the purpose for which it was provided, to communicate with you, and to maintain reasonable records of website enquiries. We do not sell personal information.

Forms and email

The current website uses your device’s email application to prepare messages addressed to hemamadhayam@gmail.com. Your email provider’s own privacy policy also applies to the transmission and storage of those messages.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies. If analytics or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them.

External links

The website may link to external services such as social networks or WhatsApp. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask what personal information from a website enquiry we hold, or to request correction of inaccurate information, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hemamadhayam@gmail.com.
